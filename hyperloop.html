Hyperloop Manchester

2022–2023

I was a member of Hyperloop Manchester, a magnetic levitation train society made up of around 80 members. I started on the Magnetic Levitation sub team, working on the Halbach array and choice of magnets to make the pod levitate. I also provided materials consultation for the other subteams. From July 2022, I became General Secretary and co head of the full scale research team.

I restructured the society as General Secretary, I rebuilt the administration system from scratch, handled communications across the subteams, chaired weekly committee meetings, set agendas and targets. I coordinated presentations and speaking engagements for the society with team leads and guest speakers. I also organised the 2023 trip the European Hyperloop week in Edinburgh.

My second role in the society was within the full scale research team where we studied tunnel design and vacuum limited. The work we did was in preparation for a presentation at European Hyperloop Week, and I was focussed on the structural and materials problems of constructing a tube under vacuum. The dominant issue is vacuum buckling. A thin walled cylinder would collapse long before the material itself yields in near vacuum condition. This means we modeled the critical buckling pressure as a function of tube geometry and materials properties to predict the point of failure. Tunnel sizing is also constrained by the Kantrowitz limit, which is a choking condition that limits how much of the cross section the pod can occupy at close to sonic speeds. I evaluated steel alloys as the primary candidate and how heat treatments and manufacturing processes affect long term performance in a near vacuum.

I also compared overground and underground systems, looking at the effect of thermal expansion, weather and seismic activity. This also includes expansion of joints, pylon systems and how these can be used to manage structural failure. The other members of my team worked on a vacuum pump system, which could be used to hold roughly 1% of atmospheric pressure across a tube including pump selection, spacing, leakage rates and depressuring cycles.

I attended European Hyperloop both in Delft in 2022 and in Edinburgh 2023. At Edinburgh we entered the Full Scale technical award in the infrastructure category and delivered a formal presentation to an expert jury and other teams on our tunnel and materials research.
